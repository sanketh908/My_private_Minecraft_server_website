Server Rules

A few simple rules so everyone has a great time.

1

Be Respectful

Treat everyone with kindness. No griefing, stealing, or harassing other players.

2

No Cheating

Keep it survival. No hacking, x-ray, or exploiting bugs that ruin the game for others.

3

Clean Up After Yourself

Fill in creeper holes, don't leave floating trees, and keep the world tidy.

4

Have Fun Together

This is a shared world. Build together, share resources, and make memories.
